## Rights and claims review

- Default bundled sounds, artwork, logos and promotional media: list each asset, source/license, permitted regions and proof of rights.
- User supplied sounds are not automatically cleared for redistribution or marketing. Store language must describe user-chosen sounds without suggesting unauthorized extraction from third-party services.
- Avoid claims to record all apps. Android source apps may prohibit playback capture.
