# Google Play listing draft

**Status:** Working draft for owner and rights review. No listing has been submitted.

## Short description

Play your own sound clips from a customizable soundboard.

## Full description

Stimmyz keeps your soundboard and Library on your Android device. Import an audio file, record your voice, or capture playback from a compatible app after Android's consent prompt; trim a short section and save it for offline playback. Arrange sounds across boards, adjust pad and playback settings, and choose artwork from your Gallery.

- Build and rename boards, and arrange their pads.
- Import supported audio files with Android's file picker.
- Record a voice clip after choosing the microphone action and granting permission.
- Capture eligible device playback after choosing the capture action and granting Android's MediaProjection consent.
- Trim clips before saving them to your private Library.
- Adjust playback options, themes and pad artwork.
- Export or restore a backup when you choose a destination.

Imported files, recordings, captured audio, board settings and selected artwork are stored locally by default. Device playback capture depends on Android consent and whether the source app permits capture. Verify these claims against the new capture-enabled candidate before submission; historical r3 does not include this feature.

## Claims to keep out of the listing

- “Record any app” or claims that all playback can be captured.
- Claims to download or extract music/sounds from YouTube, social networks or protected streaming apps.
- Cloud sync, automatic online backup or account features.
- “Private” or “secure” guarantees beyond the actual local storage and permissions behavior.

## Review instructions

1. Start with an empty local board.
2. Use Import audio or video to select a supported local audio file, trim it, save it and play it from the board.
3. Use Record your voice to see the contextual microphone prompt, record in the foreground, trim, save and play.
4. Open Settings → About → Privacy on this device for the local data explanation.
5. Use backup export/restore only with a folder chosen by the reviewer.
6. Demonstrate the scoped device playback capture flow and Android consent using the final capture-enabled candidate; do not claim it can record every app.
