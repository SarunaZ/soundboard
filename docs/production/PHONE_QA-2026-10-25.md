# Physical phone QA — 25 October 2026

This record documents the in-place upgrade QA performed with local test-key copies of r3 and r4 on one Samsung device. It is not a complete device matrix, release approval, or Google Play signing/install test. The owner's final candidate scope requires file import, microphone, and device-audio capture. Historical r3 has capture disabled; r4 enables capture. R4 install, restore retry, consent, synthetic capture-to-save, audible preview, notification Stop, no-usable-audio rejection, and device cleanup have been checked. Raw device logs and screenshots are kept in ignored local data folders and are not linked here.
