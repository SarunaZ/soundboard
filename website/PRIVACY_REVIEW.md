# Stimmyz beta privacy policy — verification gates for approval

**Status: OPEN / owner review.** This checklist records unresolved verification work. Statements in the draft are supplied policy content, not findings that these gates have been completed. Do not publish this document or use it for Play Console declarations until the items below are verified against the exact distributed build and current provider arrangements.

1. **Controller:** Confirm the exact legal controller identification and whether `Šarūnas Zubė, operating as Stimmyz` and the public contact email are appropriate. Determine whether an additional business/postal contact is required; do not publish a private home address by default.
2. **Email:** Test mail received at `privacy@stimmyz.app` and `support@stimmyz.app` from an unrelated mailbox. Test a branded reply. Confirm the actual outgoing provider (Brevo or otherwise), what recipient From/Reply-To headers reveal, and whether any personal account address leaks. Add the actual outgoing processor to section 8 before launch.
3. **Correspondence retention:** Adopt a real support/rights correspondence retention policy and deletion workflow, plus any statutory retention exception. Then replace the generic criteria if a more precise duration is available.
4. **Hosting and routing:** Check the Vercel account's log retention, contract/subprocessors and cross-border data-transfer information; check Cloudflare DNS/email forwarding and Google mailbox arrangement. Reflect verified facts rather than inventing a fixed 30/90-day period.
5. **Historical QA analytics:** Identify any consented events sent by older testing builds; verify PostHog project plan, retention and access/deletion options for those historical events. The current release's hard-OFF transport does not erase earlier QA events.
6. **Release identity and Data Safety:** Verify the final signed AAB and every other active Play testing track. If analytics is enabled in any distributed build, this analytics-OFF policy is insufficient. Reconcile Play Data Safety declarations with all active signed artifacts.
7. **Device QA, sharing and deletion:** Run real-device checks of microphone, playback capture, ZIP exports, Library deletion, Android backup and share-sheet pathways. Verify the exact all-active-build behavior and do not overclaim OEM backup prevention.
8. **Age and distribution:** Confirm Play Console audience and country choices. This draft is designed for an intended 16+ EU beta with analytics disabled; it does not claim users' ages are verified.
9. **Website, approval and publication:** Verify the Vercel/Cloudflare and email provider statements and relevant retention/transfer details. Obtain owner approval, set the actual publication date, and only then remove the draft banner and set `noIndex` to false. Verify the live page returns HTTP 200, is accessible without login, and the in-app policy link opens it. Do not deploy before owner approval.
