# StimyzApp icon

Created with the built-in image generation tool. The source mark uses real alpha
transparency. Launcher assets are deterministic size, background, and alpha-mask
derivatives of that mark.

## Generation prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand
 Asset type: production app icon foreground mark for StimyzApp, an Android soundboard for joyful vocal stimming and repeating favorite sounds.
 Primary request: create one bold, distinctive, friendly S monogram that feels like a bouncy vocal sound-wave. A single chunky, rounded, flowing S-shaped ribbon, with two short rounded sound-vibration strokes on either side. The S should be unmistakable and playful, expressing the satisfying rhythm of repeating a sound. Keep the silhouette extremely simple and readable at 32 pixels.
 Style/medium: polished contemporary flat graphic logo, smooth vector-like edges, very subtly dimensional curves, restrained clean finish.
 Color palette: bright mint matching the existing app accent #7ae3bf, with very subtle pale mint highlights. Designed to sit on existing app charcoal #14161a.
 Composition/framing: one centered isolated mark on a truly transparent background. Square 1024 x 1024 image. Mark plus vibration strokes occupy roughly the central 65 percent of the canvas, generous equal transparent margins. No surrounding tile or container. No shadow outside the symbol.
 Text: no words or lettering other than the abstract S monogram; do not spell the app name.
 Constraints: actual alpha transparency; only one icon, no mockup, no presentation sheet, no phone, no border, no watermark, no music notes, no microphone, no puzzle pieces, no infinity sign, no tiny details. A finished usable icon asset.
```
